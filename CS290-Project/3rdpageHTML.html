Here are some tips and tricks about living with a Sheepadoodle:

    TIPS

  • Always go on LONG walks!

  • Shaving your dog will help cool them

    • down in the summer

  • Make sure they have plenty of water

  • Brushing will help with grooming

  • Always keep more than one toy

    TRICKS

  • Teach your dog to stay and sit

  • Always keep towels around, they can be messy

  • Get ready to cuddle!

  • Treats are a way to a dogs heart

  • Stick to a haircut routine, stay clean!

  • Use peanut butter to distract your dog
