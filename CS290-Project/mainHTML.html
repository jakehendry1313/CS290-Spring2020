Here's some fun and interesting facts about Baloo!

  • Baloo is a Sheepadoodle, a mixed designer breed an Old English Sheepdog and Standard Party Poodle

  • Baloo's name comes from the bear, Baloo, in the book/movie, The Jungle Book!

  • Baloo loves going on walks, car rides, and of course, barking at cats from the window

  • Interesting fact: Baloo has a belly button...which is actually a result from a hernia procedure

  • Baloo also has acne on his tail, which is unfortunate at times
